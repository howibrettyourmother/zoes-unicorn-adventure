# Zoe's Unicorn Adventure 🦄🌈

A sparkly, totally wholesome one-touch flying game for Zoe (4). Zoe (with her sparkly crown) rides a fluffy rainbow unicorn through candy land, leaving a rainbow trail behind her.

**Play:** https://howibrettyourmother.github.io/zoes-unicorn-adventure/

- **Controls for little hands:** touch & drag up/down (or just tap where you want to fly). Tap a grumpy rain cloud to *boop* it with Zoe's magic wand – or fly right into it. If the finger comes off, the unicorn gently drifts toward the next treat.
- **No reading, no game over:** a little rain only costs a heart; hearts grow back, and if they run out a *Rainbow Hug* refills them.
- **Collect:** glitter stars, lollipops, cupcakes, rainbow gems and butterflies (who then fly along with you). Glitter bursts on every pickup.
- **Power-ups:** Rainbow Dash (zoom + invincible rainbow), Magnet Sparkles, Giant Bubble.
- **Levels (~2.5 min):** Cotton Candy Clouds → Lollipop Forest → Rainbow Waterfall → Starry Night Castle, ending with a stars screen and a disco dance party at the castle (tap for fireworks).
- **Pick colours:** unicorn mane (rainbow, pink, purple, blue, mint, gold) and Zoe's dress.
- **Sound:** an original bubbly candy-pop tune made live with WebAudio (music box, glockenspiel, bubbly synth bass, sparkle arps, handclaps) plus an original sung-on-the-beat chant – *"Sprinkle! Sparkle! Lollipop! Yum yum!"* – and warm voice lines. The music and words are original, not copied from any existing song. Works with the iPhone silent switch on.

Built as one self-contained `index.html` (`src/build.py`), voices from Piper TTS (`src/gen.sh`), tested headless at iPhone 13 / iPad sizes (`src/test.py`, `src/fullrun.py`).
